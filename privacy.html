Privacy Policy for MantraMala

Effective Date: December 3, 2025

Introduction

MantraMala ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the MantraMala mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The App operates entirely on your device. All data (such as your mantra count and settings) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Your current mantra count
  • Your count goal/target
  • App settings and preferences

This data remains on your device and is only accessible to you. It is not backed up to any cloud service by the App.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Permissions

The App does not request any special permissions from your device.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users. The App is suitable for all ages.
