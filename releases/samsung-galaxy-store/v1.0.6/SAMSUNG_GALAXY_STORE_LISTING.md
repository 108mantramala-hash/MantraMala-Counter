## Important Notes

### Samsung Galaxy Store vs Google Play Store Differences

**Advantages of Samsung Galaxy Store:**
1. **Less Competition**: Smaller app ecosystem means better discoverability
2. **Samsung Device Users**: Direct access to Samsung's massive user base
3. **Pre-installed**: Galaxy Store comes pre-installed on all Samsung devices
4. **Regional Strength**: Strong presence in certain markets (Korea, Asia)
5. **Promotion Opportunities**: Featured apps, editor's choice

**Considerations:**
1. **Review Process**: May take 3-7 business days
2. **Requirements**: May have stricter content guidelines
3. **Updates**: Maintain version consistency across both stores
4. **Monetization**: Same monetization options as Play Store

### Samsung Seller Portal
- **Portal URL**: https://seller.samsungapps.com/
- **Account Required**: Samsung account with seller registration
- **Registration Fee**: Free (no registration or annual fees)
- **Revenue Share**: 70/30 split (if monetizing in future)

### Best Practices
1. Keep description clear and concise
2. Use high-quality screenshots showing actual app UI
3. Respond promptly to user reviews
4. Update regularly to maintain visibility
5. Localize content for key markets
6. Highlight Samsung-specific optimizations
