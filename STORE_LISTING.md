## Full Description

🙏 **MantraMala - Your Spiritual Companion**

MantraMala is a beautifully designed mantra counting app for spiritual practitioners. Whether you practice japa meditation, chant mantras, or engage in repetitive prayer, MantraMala provides an elegant and intuitive way to track your spiritual practice.

✨ **Key Features:**

📿 **Simple Counting**
- Tap to count each mantra
- Or enable "Tap Anywhere" mode for effortless counting
- Large, easy-to-use interface

🎯 **Traditional Presets**
- Quick access to traditional counts: 27, 54, 108
- Set custom daily targets
- Visual progress tracking with beautiful circular counter

📊 **Track Your Progress**
- See your total lifetime mantras
- Monitor daily target completion
- Clean, motivating statistics

🎨 **Premium Design**
- Beautiful dark theme with gradient effects
- Smooth animations and polished UI
- Distraction-free interface perfect for meditation

⚙️ **Customizable**
- Optional sound effects and haptic feedback
- Adjustable volume
- Personalize your practice

🔒 **Privacy Focused**
- All data stored locally on your device
- No ads, no tracking, no account required
- Your practice remains private

Perfect for:
- Japa meditation practitioners
- Buddhist mala counting
- Hindu mantra chanting
- Christian rosary prayer
- Islamic dhikr counting
- Any repetitive spiritual practice

Download MantraMala today and bring mindfulness to your spiritual journey! 🌟
